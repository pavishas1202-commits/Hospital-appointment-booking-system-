# Hospital-appointment-booking-system-
Project statement Traditional hospital appointment systems often rely on manual booking methods, leading to long waiting times, scheduling conflicts, and inefficient record management. Patients may face difficulties in obtaining appointments, while hospital staff spend significant time managing schedules manually.

To provide an online platform for appointment booking.
To reduce patient waiting time.
To improve hospital management efficiency.
To maintain accurate patient and appointment records.
To provide easy access to doctor schedules.
